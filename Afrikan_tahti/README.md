# Afrikan tähti - Africa's star
Africa's star is a game where 2-5 players travel across a map to find the africa's star and deliver it to one of the starting locations
## rules:
### start
Each player gets 300 money at the start of the game. And can choose a between two starting nodes: Tangier or Cairo.
### movement
In their turns player rolls a dice and moves the amount on the board. Moving on a road is always free and you can stop at a city even if you have moves left. Sea travel is split in to 2 different modes: with or without money. If player has money it has to pay 100 and move as road nodes until next city, if player do not have money they can move 2 nodes for free per turn, no dice roll. Air travel costs 300 money and you instantly travel to the destination and can open a token if there is one, but no additional movement is available that turn.

### tokens
If a player reaches a city that has a token, they can pay 100 to open the token or try to roll 4,5,6 with the dice. When player finds the africa's star player they can win by delivering it to one of the starting areas, but after the star is found other players can join the race to win by finding a horseshoe. Before the star is found the horseshoes do not have any value. Star or horseshoe cannot be lost to a bandit. Other token values are topaz - 300, smaragd - 600, ruby - 1000, bandit - lose all money, blank - 0.

### special locations
Bandit nodes: if player has to stay at a bandit node they get stunned until they roll 1 or 2
Gold coast: gems found at this location have double reward
Slave coast: if player finds blank at this location they are stunned for 3 turns.
Cape town: first player to get to cape town receive 500 money
